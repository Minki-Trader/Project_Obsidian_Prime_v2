## Grok Review — F74 Stage Open

**Advice classification(조언 분류): `accepted(수용)`**

From the bounded snapshot alone, F74 is sufficiently **novel(신규)** and **bounded(경계 있음)** to open after F73. It does not look like a thin rename of F73.

### Why accepted

**1. Real hypothesis shift, not F73 repeat**
F73 failed on **density(밀도)** and **validation DD(검증 손실폭)** even with **perfect parity(완전 동등성)**. F74 moves the primary lever to **label/target design(라벨/목표 설계)** — **microburst first-touch(마이크로버스트 선도달)**, **3/6/9 bar horizons(3/6/9봉 수평선)**, **ATR-native targets( ATR 네이티브 목표)** — instead of **adapter/session/model rotation(어댑터/세션/모델 회전)**. That matches F73’s **negative memory(부정 기억)** without reopening the same repair loop.

**2. Respects prior “do not repeat” lines**
- F68: **path-adverse veto inside label/selection(라벨/선택 내부 경로 불리 이동 차단)** is framed as **pre-selection(선택 전)**, not **after-the-fact risk repair(사후 위험 수리)**.
- F69: **failure criteria(실패 기준)** explicitly block **threshold/cooldown/quota-only(임계값/쿨다운/할당 단독)** explanations.
- F73: **density objective(밀도 목표)** is a first-class scout variable, not a parity follow-up.

**3. Stage-open boundaries are adequate**
Success/failure criteria are concrete enough for **early scout(초기 탐색)** without smuggling in **completion/baseline/promotion/runtime authority(완성/기준선/승격/런타임 권위)**. **MT5 runtime probe(MT5 런타임 탐침)** is conditional, which fits **progressive hardening(점진적 경화)**.

**4. Not rejected**
The direction is coherent with F73’s clue (**parity alone is insufficient(동등성만으로는 부족)**) and attacks the documented gap (**lifecycle compression + density(생명주기 압축 + 밀도)**) at the label layer.

**5. Not `needs_local_verification` for the open/novelty question**
Overlap with F72 (**trade-shape labels(거래 형태 라벨)**) is plausible but not decisive from this snapshot; F74’s **turnover + native density + short-horizon first-touch(회전율 + 네이티브 밀도 + 짧은 수평선 선도달)** bundle is a distinct stated delta. Local verification can wait until implementation, not before stage open.

### One drift risk(드리프트 위험)

**Density quota backdoor(밀도 할당량 우회)** — If **microburst labels(마이크로버스트 라벨)** stay sparse after **lifecycle simulation(생명주기 시뮬레이션)**, scout pressure on **5/7/9 trades/day(일 5/7/9거래)** may quietly slide into **F69-style threshold/cooldown/daily-quota tuning( F69식 임계값/쿨다운/일별 할당 조정)** while still calling it “label-native density.”

### One repair priority(수리 우선순위)

**Label-only density gate first(라벨 단독 밀도 게이트 우선)** — Before **model family(모델 계열)** or **feature ablations(피처 제거 실험)**, prove that **raw microburst first-touch candidates(원시 마이크로버스트 선도달 후보)** — pre-lifecycle, pre-model — can reach **≥2–4 trades/day(일 2~4거래 이상)** on validation/OOS proxy. If that fails, stop and revise the label hypothesis; do not compensate with selection mechanics or another adapter pass.
