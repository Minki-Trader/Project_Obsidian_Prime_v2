- **Hypothesis axis:** selection economics (선택 경제성) — what makes a candidate *meaningful* under joint density + PF + DD constraints, not just parity-correct at runtime.
- **Separate two problems explicitly:**

  2. **Model economics** — unsolved; F70B/C zero meaningful is the blocking signal.
- **Concrete rotation:** move from “regime/session asymmetric labels + density-aware selection on current surface” to **economics-first label and selection objective** — e.g. labels tied to realized lifecycle PnL/survival under target frequency band, with explicit sparse-vs-dense joint gate before any runtime probe.
